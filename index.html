The tools

One system, four building blocks

Each tool works on its own, but they are designed to work together: towers detect, the AI decides, drones go and look — and everything reports to one dashboard.

Drone station

SkyNest Drone Station

An autonomous docking and charging station that keeps a drone ready around the clock. When a sensor raises an alert — or on a programmed schedule — the drone launches by itself, flies to the spot, streams live video and returns to charge.

  • Automatic launch, landing & charging
  • Weather-protected housing
  • Scheduled patrols or alert-triggered flights
  • Live video to control room
  • Inspection & verification only — no offensive capability
  • Remote software updates
Fixed surveillance

Multi-Sensor Tower

A modular mast carrying radar, lidar and EO/IR (day and thermal) cameras. It watches a perimeter continuously and spots people, vehicles, drones or animals long before they reach the fence — day, night, fog or snow.

  • Radar + lidar + EO/IR cameras
  • Continuous perimeter coverage
  • Rapid deployment, modular build
  • All-weather, Nordic-rated
  • AI classification cuts false alarms
  • Triggers SkyNest drones automatically
Patrol drone

Robocopter — Autonomous Patrol

An autonomous multicopter that flies programmed routes over large areas. Used for wide-area monitoring and for humane wildlife deterrence: flying like a bird of prey, it moves flocks of geese off farmland without touching them.

  • Programmed patrol routes
  • Sensor-based detection
  • Bird-of-prey flight pattern
  • Covers large fields & sites
  • Harmless to protected species
  • No continuous human labour
Logistics

SkyCarrier — Cargo Drone

A heavier-lift drone for moving spare parts, samples and supplies to places that are slow or costly to reach by road — remote installations, islands, field crews and sites cut off by snow or flooding.

  • Point-to-point cargo flights
  • Reaches remote & off-road sites
  • Replaces costly vehicle trips
  • Works with the same docking network

FAQ

Common questions

Do I need a drone pilot?

No. Drones launch, fly and land autonomously from the SkyNest station. Operations are supervised remotely in line with aviation rules.

Are the drones armed or able to intervene?

No. Rumble Tools systems are for detection, inspection and verification only — they have no offensive capability.

Is goose deterrence legal for a protected species?

Yes. The barnacle goose is protected under the EU Birds Directive, so methods must not harm the birds. Our drones only scare flocks away by their presence and flight pattern, with no physical contact.

Does it work in winter?

The systems are designed for Nordic conditions — darkness, snow and frost. Thermal cameras and radar keep working when ordinary cameras cannot see.

Can I buy, lease or order it as a service?

Contact us — we tailor the model to your site, whether that is a one-off deployment, a seasonal service (e.g. goose season) or a long-term surveillance contract.
